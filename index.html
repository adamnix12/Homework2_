Reshiram

This legendary Pokémon can scorch the world with fire. It helps those who want to build a world of truth. Reshiram can be fused together with the legendary Pokemon Kyurem using a DNA splicer. This was introduced in Pokemon White Version 2.

Stats

  1. Type: Dragon & Fire
  2. HP: 100
  3. Speed: 90
  4. Attack: 120
  5. Defense: 100

Moves

  • Flamethrower
  • Dragon Claw
  • Dragon Rage
  • Fusion Flare
